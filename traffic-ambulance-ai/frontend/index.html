MODULES

One pipeline, five decisions made in real time.

01

Detect & Track

YOLOv11 classifies ten vehicle types per frame; ByteTrack keeps a stable ID on every one across occlusions.

02

Measure Density & Speed

Per-lane occupancy, flow rate, and speed are recomputed every frame to grade congestion Low → Critical.

03

Prioritize Emergencies

The moment an ambulance is confirmed, its lane, speed, and ETA drive a live green corridor across four signals.

04

Catch Accidents

Collision, overturn, and stationary-vehicle heuristics raise an alert and reroute traffic before it backs up.

BUILT ON

A stack that ships, not a demo that stalls.

FastAPI + PostgreSQL + WebSocket backend, YOLOv11 with ByteTrack for perception, Chart.js and Leaflet on the front end. This console runs in simulation mode until it's pointed at that backend — see the connection panel inside.
